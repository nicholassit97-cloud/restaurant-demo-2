Our Story

Since 2005, Selera Malaysia has been serving the finest authentic Malaysian dishes. From Penang Laksa to Nasi Kerabu, we bring the diverse flavors of Malaysia to your table.


Our chefs use only the freshest ingredients and traditional recipes passed down through generations, ensuring an authentic taste of Malaysia in every dish.
